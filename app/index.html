Pebble Town

Build a town, then go inside

Grown-ups: swipe the street to scroll the town, or tap the map to see the whole town and jump anywhere. Tap the hammer (or an empty lot) to build houses and shops, tap a building to go inside, and the back arrow to return to town. Drag a friend onto a building, a room button or the back arrow to take them along. Drop a friend into a car to go for a ride, and tap a traffic light to stop the cars. On iPad, tap Share, then “Add to Home Screen” for full-screen play. The town is saved on this device. version __BUILD__
